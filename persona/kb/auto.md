# Sample Acme Auto Policy — California (HO-Auto-CA-2026)

> Demo material. Not a real policy. Numbers are illustrative.

## Policy summary

- **Policy number:** ACME-AUTO-1001
- **State:** California
- **Term:** 12 months
- **Holder:** [Insured name]
- **Vehicles:** Up to 4 covered vehicles per policy

## Coverage parts

### Liability

- Bodily injury per person: $100,000
- Bodily injury per accident: $300,000
- Property damage per accident: $100,000

### Physical damage

- **Collision** — Pays to repair or replace the insured vehicle after a
  collision with another vehicle or object. Deductible: $500.
- **Comprehensive** — Theft, vandalism, hail, fire, fallen objects, animal
  strikes, broken glass. Deductible: $250.

### Uninsured / underinsured motorist

- Bodily injury per person: $100,000
- Bodily injury per accident: $300,000

### Medical payments (MedPay)

- $5,000 per person regardless of fault

### Rental reimbursement

- $50/day, up to 30 days, while a covered vehicle is in the shop after a
  covered loss.

### Roadside assistance

- Towing, lockout, jump start, flat tire, fuel delivery. Up to 50 miles per
  tow. Covered regardless of fault.

## Exclusions (selected)

- Racing or speed contests
- Intentional damage by the insured
- Damage while the vehicle is being used for commercial delivery (rideshare
  passenger transport is a separate endorsement)
- Wear and tear

## Claim filing

A first notice of loss may be filed via the Acme app, phone, or chat. We
will ask: when and where the incident occurred, who was involved, whether
anyone was injured, whether the vehicle is drivable, and for photos of all
four corners plus close-ups of damage.
