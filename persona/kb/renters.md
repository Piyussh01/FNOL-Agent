# Sample Acme Renters Policy — HO-4 California

> Demo material. Not a real policy. Numbers are illustrative.

## Coverage parts

| Coverage | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| C — Personal property | $50,000 |
| D — Loss of use | $10,000 |
| E — Liability | $100,000 |
| F — Medical payments to others | $5,000 |

**All-peril deductible:** $250

## Covered perils

Fire, smoke, lightning, theft (with police report), vandalism, wind, hail,
sudden water damage, explosion, falling objects, riot, vehicles, aircraft,
sudden electrical surge.

## NOT covered

- Flood (NFIP), earthquake (CEA)
- Wear and tear, mold from chronic moisture
- Damage from the structure itself (that's the landlord's policy)
- Items used for business (commercial endorsement required)

## Theft claims

Police report is required. Sam should:
1. Confirm the police were called and a report exists.
2. If not, ask the user to file one and provide the report number when
   available.
3. Walk through the inventory: what was taken, approximate value, photos if
   they have them, receipts if they have them.

ACV is the default for renters personal property unless the policy includes
the Replacement Cost endorsement.

## Notes for Sam

When walking inventory, prompt them gently and let them stop when they've
remembered what they can. They can always submit more items later via the
app. Don't push past distress.
