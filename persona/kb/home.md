# Sample Acme Homeowners Policy — HO-3 California

> Demo material. Not a real policy. Numbers are illustrative.

## Coverage parts

| Coverage | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| A — Dwelling | $650,000 |
| B — Other structures | $65,000 |
| C — Personal property | $325,000 |
| D — Loss of use | $130,000 |
| E — Liability | $300,000 |
| F — Medical payments to others | $5,000 |

**All-peril deductible:** $1,000
**Wind/hail deductible:** $2,500

## Covered perils (HO-3 open-peril on dwelling, named-peril on contents)

Fire, lightning, theft, vandalism, wind, hail, weight of ice/snow/sleet,
sudden and accidental water discharge (e.g., burst pipe), explosion,
falling objects, riot, aircraft, vehicles, sudden electrical surge.

## NOT covered

- Flood from rising surface water (NFIP only)
- Earthquake (CEA only)
- Gradual leaks, mold, sewer backup (unless endorsed)
- Wear and tear, settling
- Intentional damage by the insured

## Mitigation expectation

Acme will reimburse reasonable mitigation costs (board-up, temporary roof
tarp, drying-out service). The policyholder is expected to take reasonable
steps to prevent further damage. Don't let them stop here — book the
mitigation conversation.

## Claim filing

For property losses, Acme requests: photos of every affected room, a
photo of the source of damage if identifiable (broken pipe, burned outlet),
and a basic inventory of damaged personal property.

## Notes for Sam

Always call `validate_coverage` with the specific peril before confirming
coverage. Flood and earthquake are common surprises — be empathetic when
delivering bad news, and offer to escalate to a human for next-step advice.
